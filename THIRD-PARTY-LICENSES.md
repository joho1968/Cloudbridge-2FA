## Simple.css

Paths: [simple.css](cloudbridge-2fa/css/simple.css) and [simple.min.css](cloudbridge-2fa/css/simple.min.css)
Upstream: https://github.com/kevquirk/simple.css
License: [MIT](cloudbridge-2fa/css/LICENSE)

Simple.css provides base styles for the OTP browser screen and HTML e-mail.
